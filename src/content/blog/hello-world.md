## Writing a post

Add a `.md` file to `src/content/blog/` with a title and publication date in its frontmatter. The filename becomes the URL: `hello-world.md` lives at `/blog/hello-world`.

- Write regular Markdown.
- Use headings to organize your thoughts.
- Typeset handles the typography.

> Keep the setup simple and let the words do the work.
